## Outcome and decision

**Outcome: met for implementation and evaluation; product results are mixed.**
Recommend **promote the exact frozen contract combination** as a bounded,
reversible practical improvement. The substantive gain is source-directed DOE
investigation and use of actual eligibility criteria. Fresh questions, correct
inference and simple lookups survive. This recommendation accepts three extra
ordinary model requests and 88,496 extra input tokens in this sample; it is not
a general research-economy or isolated Answer-prompt improvement claim.

Klamath completion coverage remains materially incomplete. Sparse research does
not stop more economically, and its revised final adds a minor unsupported lead.
These failures were preserved without a second prompt refinement. They do not
erase the specific DOE gain, but prevent a broad competence, chronology,
grounding-reliability or stopping-success claim. Promotion remains a human review
decision; no publication or merge was performed, and no untested hybrid is proposed.

The full local review bundle is
`C:\tmp\scryraven-prompt-contract-reset-01`; exact questions, inputs, returned
decisions, public source text, usage receipts and all negative results remain
separate from runtime. This report does not claim general reliability or causal

## Intervention and external grounding

Only the Research and Answer instruction literals and native Find/Read/calculator
descriptions change. Schemas, role assignments, provider behavior, tools, limits,
session state, citation custody and Reading Room are unchanged. Delegated Research
still appends its existing need-specific instruction to the same Research contract.
No agent, reviewer, checklist, planning lifecycle, evidence-processing layer,
provider router, persistent state or alternate product path is added.

The supplied audit and candidate files were design inputs, not execution
instructions. The candidates were adapted to retain explicit user adoption and
conditional-premise handling, material references in briefings, comparison bases,
delivery/citation protocol, and faithful optional source descriptions. No named
evaluation case, case fact, source URL or worked example enters runtime prompts.

[OpenAI reasoning guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices)
motivates direct outcomes, clear sections and boundaries, zero-shot evaluation
before examples, and no requested private reasoning transcript.
[Function-calling guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling)
motivates truthful purpose, syntax and output descriptions. These are design
hypotheses; vendor advice does not demonstrate application improvement. The
existing two roles retain their judgment and a useful partial remains legitimate.

| Find | “Find exact text” implies exhaustive exact-string matching. | Lexically rank retained regions; return bounded original passages with nearby context. Selection is capped at eight before coalescing and attention delivery. A miss or omission is not a semantic absence finding. |
| Research Read | Acquisition and exposure can be confused. | `auto` on an exact E ID rereads that item, including a highlight. `full` reuses or acquires the latest full parent by URL, then large parents expose bounded lexical views. `local` never fetches; exact ranges address acquired text. |

| Calculator | Scalar description omitted the actual grammar. | Decimal literals, `+ - * /` and parentheses; one expression per call. A valid example and decimal replacement for scientific notation are explicit. Exponents, functions, units and collections remain unsupported. |

### Evidence-use and stopping attribution

DOE's improvement occurs before and through Answer: revised Research changes
direction, performs two successful full-source Reads and selects actual Appendix A
criteria; Answer uses those originals rather than merely repeating a confident
briefing. The original performs three Searches and continues the neighboring
worker-authorization interpretation. Differing acquired evidence precludes an
Answer-only causal claim. The lean fixed Answer comparison independently shows
that both Answer contracts can use a supplied permit exclusion.

The revised handbook parent E34 contains 333,439 characters. Its actual foreword,
explicit nonmandatory statement and nuclear-scope/broader-use paragraphs were
acquired but never exposed to either role. Research saw E34 ranges 0:3154
(containing a table-of-contents foreword entry), 49690:59439 and 182814:201896;
Answer received the latter two. Diagnostic parent coordinates 9704:10041,
14217:14254 and 14255:14854 identify the omitted scope text, not exposed aliases.
The answer preserves a general handbook-recommendations versus governing-site-rules
distinction, but not that specific applicability context. This is an
acquired-but-unexposed scope opportunity with no subsequent inspection, not
proof that Answer ignored supplied text.

Neither Klamath run acquired the missing four-California-tributary completion
account. Both acquired a different 2026 retrospective about Oregon restoration;
that material does not supply the missing California completion and cannot be
silently presented as a report available by end-2025. No false four-versus-five
tributary completion claim was made. The main failure remains acquisition/update
coverage, rather than the unavailable historical exposed-but-unused failure.
Revised Answer made four local library Reads without obtaining the missing fact.
Its shorter time is therefore not credited as improved economy.

Sparse revised Research initially returned after 13 external attempts. Answer
delegated again, spending the remaining three before Research handed back its
unresolved result. Both arms ultimately used all 16 attempts; neither was forced
out by a physical-request or time bound. Revised used nine Research and six Answer
requests, including five native Answer continuations. The employer lead came from
a Serper caption in catalog candidate C32: navigation only, with no acquired
material IDs. Reading C32 produced paywalled E40 without the caption. None of the
68 acquired source bodies co-mentions this person and employer. Research explicitly
recognized that boundary, but carried the caption through its fallible briefing;
final Answer repeated it cautiously without support. Final selected originals are
LinkedIn versions only. This is navigation-to-briefing-to-answer leakage, not
evidence that the lead is false or a model-memory fabrication. The qualification
prevents a confident employment claim but does not ground the association.

The revised fixed stopping decision thus failed to transfer to product economy.
No further tuning, selective retest, source insertion or runtime repair followed
these negative observations. Both controls and failures remain in the complete
comparison rather than selecting only a preferred answer.
