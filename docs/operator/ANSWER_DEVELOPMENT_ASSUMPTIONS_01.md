## MD80 decision

Both candidate draws preserved refusal of unsupported direct subtraction and did
not invent load factors, assign carrier-wide occupancy to aircraft types, or name
an empirical winner. Both explained cost per passenger-mile through a compatible
cost denominator and the CASM/load-factor relation. The repeat used the calculator
for the 8 cents/seat-kilometer conversion to approximately 12.9 cents/seat-mile.

The material omission is not merely the missing MD80 numeral. The packet contains
a discounted fleet-replacement exercise with capital/scrap, fuel and maintenance
assumptions, alongside a 2015–2017 airline cost observation. The latter separates
4.2 direct and 3.8 indirect cents/seat-kilometer. Generic calls for the same cost
definition do not develop that actual mismatch. Both candidate answers leave the
reader without the central explanation of why occupancy alone cannot repair it.
They identify needed comparable inputs, but do not sufficiently distinguish those
empirical unknowns from optional scenario choices. The full requested positive
behavior was therefore not demonstrated across repeats.
