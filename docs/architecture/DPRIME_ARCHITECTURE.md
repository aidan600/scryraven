## Installed And Selected Status

**INSTALLED:** Component D-prime has no ordinary component producer or consumer.
The bounded genuine N>=2 path still executes a synthesis D-prime model call after
Cross-Component Analyst and before RunKernel synthesis admission.

**SELECTED TARGET:** the remaining synthesis D-prime call is a retirement target,
not permanent architecture. Its useful differentiated scrutiny should move into
Cross self-audit and risk-scaled Scrutineer only after focused PRODUCT
convergence proves that change. Deterministic graph checks and RunKernel
admission remain mandatory.

Read [AnalystOS Operating Model](ANALYSTOS_OPERATING_MODEL.md) for the selected

## Responsibility

D-prime is an evidence-relative semantic validation role for one nominated
proposal. It may assess support, applicability, contradiction, scope,
currentness meaning, caveats, and exact Specialist-result consumption against
the bounded inputs it receives.

It must not:

- invent or replace the claim or synthesis;
- search, retrieve, or create evidence;
- calculate a substitute result;
- mutate the AnswerContract or graph;
- admit canonical state;
- decide Sufficiency;
- package FAP; or
- write final prose.

A D-prime result is still proposal/validation state until RunKernel consumes the
exact current refs and admits or rejects it.

## Proposal, Validation, And Admission

Installed component path:

```text
Component Analyst case + self-audit
-> deterministic schema/identity/currentness/lineage checks
-> RunKernel admission or block
```

Installed synthesis path:

```text
Cross-Component Analyst synthesis proposal
-> synthesis D-prime validation
-> deterministic graph/identity/revision/cycle checks
-> RunKernel synthesis admission or block
```

Workers author or challenge meaning. Code binds mechanics. No worker mutates
canonical state or validates its own proposal.

Every review is bound to the exact input it evaluated. Stale contract,
component, graph, evidence, proposal, Specialist, action, or lease identity
fails closed rather than being rebound to current state.

## Evidence-Relative Review

D-prime may distinguish:

```text
semantic support: what the bounded material means for the nominated proposal
evidential adequacy: whether that support is scoped, representative, current,
and appropriate enough for the proposal
```

Custody, readability, candidate presence, model agreement, source class, or
mechanical preflight are not semantic support by themselves.

A review may support, support with caveats, abstain, challenge, identify a
contradiction, or preserve a missing premise. A follow-up need is not search
authorization.

## Specialist Consumption

A Specialist result is a bounded deterministic contribution, not self-authorizing
support. Synthesis D-prime may consume a completed handoff only when the exact
result, unit, precision, operator, assumptions, caveats, target, claim alignment,
and two-hop source lineage match the nominated synthesis.

RunKernel independently verifies the current target, handoff, action, artifact,
and consumption identity before admission. Missing or stale Specialist lineage
cannot be rescued by D-prime prose.

Source-stated literals are ordinary direct evidence and do not require D-prime or
Specialist merely because they contain numbers.

## Downstream Boundary

D-prime has no direct FAP or Author authority. FAP consumes only exact current
RunKernel-admitted state that Sufficiency authorizes.

For direct-source admitted claims, FAP checks mechanical component, observation,
Coverage, content, evidence, citation, currentness, and digest lineage. It does
not reparse claim prose, compare literal signatures, or ask D-prime to authorize
ordinary source-stated numbers.

For genuinely derived results, exact `specialist_derived_numeric` authority
remains hard. The applicable semantic owner must have consumed the Specialist
result before RunKernel admission and FAP packaging.

Author is the final semantic actor. Post-Author PRODUCT processing is mechanical
only.

## Selected Retirement Direction

A future synthesis D-prime retirement phase must prove that:

- Cross produces a defensible relationship case with self-audit;
- Scrutineer supplies any still-useful independent challenge under mode/risk
  policy;
- negative controls remain blocked;
- deterministic graph validation and RunKernel admission remain exact; and
- PRODUCT answer quality does not regress.

Do not remove the installed call merely because the selected target is simpler,
and do not add new D-prime consumers merely because the compatibility role still
exists.

## Nonproofs

This role contract does not prove arbitrary-query support, broad synthesis
quality, model reliability, retrieval quality, citation correctness, Specialist
correctness, or product answer quality. It records current role ownership and
the installed-versus-selected distinction only.
