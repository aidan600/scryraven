# ScryRaven Product

Status: transitional product charter pending first-principles product review

ScryRaven is a research-assistant project intended to turn user research questions into useful, evidence-grounded answers.

The exact future MVP and product envelope are currently under first-principles redesign.

Important future capabilities may be identified or reconsidered during that review. Their importance does not require preservation of the previous implementation machinery used to pursue them.

The existing v1 implementation creates no general compatibility or preservation obligation for a rebuild.

No ScryRaven v2 architecture is currently approved.

Do not begin v2 product implementation until Part B establishes:

- the initial supported product promise;
- concrete human-comprehensible acceptance examples;
- the first walking-skeleton / vertical-slice target.

Until then, existing implementation may be inspected deliberately as evidence, prior art, or a possible donor.

It is not the default architecture for future work.
